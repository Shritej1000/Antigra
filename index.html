01  —  The difference

Most machines make tea. Ours makes chai.

The shortcut every other system takes is concentrate — pre-brewed black tea, held warm, cut with powder. It is fast, it is cheap, and your customers can taste it. We refused that trade from the first prototype.

01

Fresh ingredients, every cup

Fresh milk, fresh water, loose leaf and spices — dosed and brewed at the moment of the order. No pre-brewed black tea. No concentrates. No powders.

02

Patented adaptive brewing

Our Adaptive Brewing System reads each batch and corrects for it, so cup nine hundred tastes like cup one — across shifts, sites and seasons.

03

Every cup is a data point

Strength, sugar and milk are configurable per cup. Every cup and batch is logged, so consumption, cost and machine health stop being guesswork.

80+

Years in retail chai, behind every unit

AntiGra started in 2016, in embedded systems and software. Two of our three founders grew up in a family that has run a retail packaged tea business for more than eighty years — and they went looking for a machine that could hold that standard at scale.

There wasn’t one. So we built it, near Mumbai, and kept building: patented brewing that produces a genuine cup in seconds, and a software layer that makes a fleet of machines legible from a single screen.

07  —  Before you ask

Straight answers.

Anything not covered here, ask us directly — contact@antigra.com.

Do you use concentrate or pre-brewed tea?
No. Not on any model. Every cup is brewed from fresh milk, fresh water, tea leaves and spices at the moment it is ordered. There is no pre-brewed black tea held warm in a tank, and no powdered concentrate anywhere in the flow path. It is the whole reason the company exists.
Can it make South Indian filter coffee too?
Yes — SuperBrew Automatic brews both chai and authentic filter coffee from fresh ingredients, which is why it is the usual choice for offices serving a mixed team. SuperBrew SA is specialised for chai and teas: chai with spice add-ons, black tea, and green tea for iced tea programmes.
How much daily maintenance does it need?
SmartBrew cleans down in under seven minutes a day, SuperBrew Automatic in under ten, and SuperBrew SA runs an automatic daily clean that takes minutes and needs no trained operator. There is no dismantling and no specialist chemistry — a pantry team handles it as part of close-down.
Can each site run a different recipe?
Yes. Strength, sugar and milk are configurable down to the individual cup, and recipes are set per machine and per location from ChaiCloud. Chains typically hold one house recipe across all sites and vary sweetness by region; that is a dashboard change, not a service visit.
What does ChaiCloud actually give me?
Live online/offline status and health for every machine, error alerts, remote diagnostics and maintenance history; cups and batches brewed by day, week and month; and analytics on usage trends, machine efficiency, machine-to-machine comparison and location-wise performance. You can walk through the real interface on the dashboard.
How much space does a unit need?
All three models are designed to fit a small footprint — an existing pantry counter or store back-bar, not a new room. Send us a photo and the counter dimensions with your enquiry and we will confirm the fit before anything is ordered.
Where are you based, and where do you deploy?
AntiGra Systems Pvt Ltd is based near Mumbai, India, where the machines are engineered and built. Units run today across Bengaluru, Mumbai, Pune and Delhi NCR with retail chains, manufacturing sites and corporate campuses.
